The four rooms

  • Guard Room (top-left) — Rin's desk and board. This is the main stage.
  • Focus Hall (top-right) — silent body-doubling desks. Work quietly alongside others.
  • Chores Corner (bottom-left) — parallel home and admin rounds.
  • Practice Dojo (bottom-right) — PracticeLoop reps and Clinic first steps.

You spawn in the central lobby. Doors are the gaps in the walls. English only. Rin is not human, and this is a pilot: the map art is simple, and there are no private rooms or moderation tools yet beyond WorkAdventure's own controls.
