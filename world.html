The office, and its four rooms

Walk with arrow keys or WASD. Everyone who enters at the same time is in the same world and can see each other. No sign-in or payment is needed.

  • Guard Room (top-left lounge) — Rin's main stage. Step in and press SPACE to join the video circle.
  • Focus Hall (central open desks) — silent zone for quiet work alongside others.
  • Practice Dojo (right meeting room) — step in and press SPACE to join the video circle for reps and Clinic first steps.
  • Chores Corner (bottom garden) — parallel home and admin rounds.

Clinic board: in the garden, press SPACE on the Clinic board to open the First-Step Clinic page. If the world does not load inside this page, use Open full screen.
