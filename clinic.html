How it works

  1. You name the stuck task in one sentence.
  2. I ask at most one clarifying question.
  3. You get one step (≤5 minutes), one if–then cue, and one smaller fallback step if you're still stuck.
  4. You reply Done, or Still stuck — and the next step gets smaller, not louder.

What you won't get: a streak that resets after one bad day, daily nagging, guilt, or a lecture. A miss shrinks the next step. At most one nudge a day, and nudges stop after one unanswered nudge.

Cách hoạt động

  1. Bạn mô tả việc đang bí trong một câu.
  2. Tôi hỏi tối đa một câu làm rõ.
