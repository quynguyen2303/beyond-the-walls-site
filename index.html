What is PracticeLoop?

Courses teach. Reps build — I have lived that my whole life. PracticeLoop turns any skill into one small daily rep, guided by AI that learns from your mistakes.

  • One rep a day. A floor-sized rep — 10 to 15 minutes. Small enough that you never skip it. Small things, done daily, are how strength is made.
  • Mistakes become traces. Every error is logged as a trace, and you fix one thing at a time — never a pile of corrections. That is how a fighter corrects form.
  • Never two misses in a row. Miss a day and the next rep shrinks to meet you. The streak bends. It does not break. Neither do you.
  • Your Skill Box. Rules distilled from your own recurring mistake patterns — drafted by AI, approved by you. Your weaknesses, turned into your guard.
